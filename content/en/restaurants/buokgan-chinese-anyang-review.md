📍 **안양시 동편마을, Korea** · 💰 10000 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 5분(테이블 수 많지 않음)

---

> *"Thick beef jjamppong and black vinegar tangsuyuk that hit the spot."*

Went to 부엌간 차이니즈 in 안양 with low expectations, left pleasantly surprised. The 우삼겹소고기짬뽕 was the star – thick, meaty broth with a deep beef flavor, not the usual watery stuff. 수란 오겹 간짜장 was a tad sweet but clean, with generous pork belly slices. 흑초 등심 탕수육 mini was perfectly sized, tender meat, and the black vinegar sauce had great balance – not too sour, not too sweet. The free 어향군만두 from Naver review? Just okay, standard fried dumplings.

Small restaurant, only a few tables – book ahead on weekdays. Dongpyeon Village vibe is cozy, parking easy. Perfect before hitting one of the many cafes nearby. Solid 4/5 for a casual Korean-Chinese fix.
