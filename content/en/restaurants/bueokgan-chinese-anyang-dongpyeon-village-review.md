📍 **안양시 동편마을, Korea** · 💰 10000 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 5분(테이블 수 많지 않음)

---

> *"Clean, no-fuss Chinese food that hits the spot after a stroll through Dongpyeon Village."*

Dropped by 부엌간 차이니즈 on a weekend trip to Dongpyeon Village. The 우삼겹소고기짬뽕 was the surprise winner – thick, meaty broth with a nice peppery kick, not the usual watery stuff. 수란 오겹 간짜장 was a bit sweet but clean, with generous pork belly slices. 흑초 등심 탕수육 (mini) was perfectly balanced – crispy, tender, and the black vinegar sauce didn't overpower. The 어향군만두 (free from Naver review) was just okay, standard pan-fried dumplings.

Small place with only a few tables, so book ahead on weekdays. Parking was easy, and after eating, you can hop to one of the many cafes nearby. Feels like a proper chef-run joint – nothing fancy, just solid execution. 4/5 for a casual weekend outing.
