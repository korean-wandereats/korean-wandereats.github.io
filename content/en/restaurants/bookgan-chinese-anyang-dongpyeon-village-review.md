📍 **안양시 동편마을, Korea** · 💰 10000 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 5분(테이블 수 많지 않음)

---

> *"A tiny Chinese joint in Dongpyeon Village that nails the basics with clean flavors."*

Dropped by 부엌간 차이니즈 in Dongpyeon Village on a weekend. Ordered the 수란 오겹 간짜장 – a bit sweet, but clean and loaded with thick pork belly slices. The 우삼겹소고기 짬뽕 surprised me: a rich, meaty broth with a thick consistency that coated every noodle. 흑초 등심 탕수육 (mini) was solid – black vinegar sauce balanced, pork tender, and portion bigger than expected. The free 어향군만두 from a Naver review? Just standard pan-fried dumplings.

Place is small, maybe 6 tables. Feels like a proper chef-run joint. Parking easy. After eating, wander to the nearby cafes in Dongpyeon Village. It's a solid weekend plan.
