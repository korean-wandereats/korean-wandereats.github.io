📍 **안양시 동편마을, Korea** · 💰 10000 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 5분(테이블 수 많지 않음)

---

> *"Solid, no-nonsense Chinese food that hits the spot without trying too hard."*

부엌간 차이니즈 in Dongpyeon Village is the kind of place you go when you want clean, satisfying Chinese food without the grease bomb. I ordered the 수란 오겹 간짜장 – slightly sweet but balanced, with thick, chewy noodles and generous chunks of pork belly. The star was the 우삼겹소고기 짬뽕: a rich, meaty broth with a velvety thickness that clung to every strand. Not your usual watery jjamppong – this one had real depth.

흑초 등심 탕수육 (mini) was surprisingly generous, with tender pork and a black vinegar sauce that hit sweet-sour notes just right. The 어향군만두? Freebie from Naver review – decent but nothing special. Small, cozy space with limited tables – book a weekday visit. Easy parking, and after eating, you can wander over to one of the many cute cafes in Dongpyeon. Solid weekend outing material.
