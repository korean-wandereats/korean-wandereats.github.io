📍 **진해, Korea** · 💰 고급모듬회 특대 150,000원, 하모회 50,000원 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 예약

---

> *"바다가 식탁 위로 올라왔다."*

Woke up craving raw fish, so I dragged my friends to 대섬횟집 in Jinhae. Ordered the 고급모듬회 특대 (150,000 won) and 하모회 (50,000 won). The moment the platter landed, I knew it was money well spent. The hwae was so fresh it barely needed ssamjang—just a dab of wasabi and soy sauce. The texture? Firm, clean, with that perfect snap. The 하모회 (conger eel) was a summer special—silky, slightly chewy, and packed with umami. Even the side seafood (abalone, shrimp, sea squirt) was top-notch. We were 5 people and left stuffed.

The view? Right on Jinhae Bay. Quiet location, spotless interior. Pro tip: book a window table. Next time, I'm trying the 장어. This is not a "hidden gem"—it's a straight-up ocean-to-table experience. If you're in Jinhae and love hwae, just go.
