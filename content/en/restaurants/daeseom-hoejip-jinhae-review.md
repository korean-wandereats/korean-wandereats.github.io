📍 **진해, Korea** · 💰 고급모듬회 특대 150,000원, 하모회 50,000원 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 예약

---

> *"Sashimi so fresh it practically jumps off the plate into your mouth."*

Dropped by 대섬횟집 in Jinhae for a late summer sashimi fix. Ordered the 고급모듬회 특대 (150,000 won) and 하모회 (50,000 won) for five of us. The premium set came with a ridiculous spread of seafood—beyond just fish, we got abalone, shrimp, and more. Everything was ice-cold and snappy. The 하모회 (conger eel) was silky, clean, and perfect for the humid weather.

Location is quiet, right by the bay with killer views. Interior is spotless, almost too clean for a fish market joint. They gave us a window table as requested—worth it. Next time, I'm trying the 장어 (eel). At 200,000 won total for five people, it's not cheap, but the quality and quantity justify it. No frills, just really good raw fish.
