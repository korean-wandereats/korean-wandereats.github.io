📍 **안양시 동편마을, Korea** · 💰 10000 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 5분(테이블 수 많지 않음)

---

> *"Clean, honest Chinese food that hits the spot without the grease."*

Finally tried 부엌간 차이니즈 in Dongpyeon Village. Got the 수란 오겹 간짜장 (sweet but clean, with thick pork belly pieces), 우삼겹소고기 짬뽕 (surprisingly good – thick meaty broth, not the usual seafood base), and 흑초 등심 탕수육 mini (black vinegar sauce was balanced and the pork tender – mini size was plenty). The 어향군만두 from a Naver review was just okay, standard pan-fried dumplings.

Small place with only a few tables – book on weekdays. Parking was easy, and after eating you can walk to any of the cute cafes nearby. Perfect for a weekend outing. 4/5.
