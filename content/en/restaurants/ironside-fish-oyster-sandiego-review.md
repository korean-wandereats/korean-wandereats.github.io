I visited Ironside Fish & Oyster in San Diego's Little Italy on April 21, 2026. The place has a hip, lively atmosphere and serves fresh seafood that pairs perfectly with white wine. I ordered the octopus a la plancha and chopped tuna toast.

The octopus a la plancha was the star – incredibly tender, with a nice smoky char from the grill. The texture was spot-on, not chewy at all, and seasoned just enough to let the natural flavor shine. It reminded me of good Korean grilled octopus but with a lighter touch.

The chopped tuna toast was a solid starter. Fresh, bright, and balanced – the tuna was chopped fine and mixed with a light dressing that didn't overpower. The toast added a nice crunch. Good umami, but I wish the portion was a bit bigger for the price.

Service was attentive, and the vibe is trendy but not pretentious. The open kitchen adds energy. Prices are on the higher side – about $25 per person for two plates – but the quality and atmosphere justify it.

One downside: the seating can be a bit cramped, especially near the bar. And if you're looking for a quiet dinner, this isn't it – the place buzzes.

Overall, Ironside is a great spot for a date or a night out with friends. Order a glass of white wine or a cocktail – they really complement the seafood. I'd go back for the octopus alone.

## Pros & Cons

**What I liked:**
- Tender, smoky octopus a la plancha
- Fresh and balanced chopped tuna toast
- Hip, lively atmosphere in Little Italy

**What could be better:**
- Slightly pricey for portion sizes
- Can be cramped and noisy

## Verdict

Ironside Fish & Oyster delivers fresh, flavorful seafood with a trendy vibe. The octopus is a must-try. Worth the splurge for a fun night out with good wine.

**Recommended for:** Seafood lovers who enjoy a lively, hip atmosphere and don't mind spending a bit more for quality.

**Skip if:** Anyone looking for a quiet, budget-friendly meal or large portions.
