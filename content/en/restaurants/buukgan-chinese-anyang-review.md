> *"The beef jjamppong at Buukgan Chinese is unexpectedly addictive."*

Finally hit Buukgan Chinese in Dongpyeon Village. Ordered the pork belly jjajangmyeon, beef jjamppong, mini black vinegar tangsuyuk, and free fried dumplings from Naver review. The jjajangmyeon was slightly sweet but clean, with generous pork belly chunks. The beef jjamppong stole the show—thick, meaty broth with deep umami, not your usual seafood version. Tangsuyuk was perfectly crispy with well-balanced black vinegar sauce. Dumplings were just okay.

Small, chef-run spot with maybe 6 tables. Weekday reservation recommended. Parking easy, and after eating, hit one of the many cafes nearby for coffee. Perfect weekend outing spot.
