📍 **안양시 동편마을, Korea** · 💰 10000 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 5분(테이블 수 많지 않음)

---

> *"Clean, no-nonsense Chinese food that hits the spot without the grease."*

Went to 부엌간 차이니즈 in 동편마을 and ordered the whole lineup. The 수란 오겹 간짜장 was slightly sweet but clean—thick pork belly pieces actually present. The 우삼겹소고기 짬뽕 surprised me: rich, meaty broth with a velvety thickness, not the usual watery stuff. 흑초 등심 탕수육 (mini) was well-balanced—black vinegar sauce not too sharp, pork tender. 어향군만두? Free from Naver review, tasted like standard pan-fried dumplings.

Small spot, only a few tables. Weekday reservation recommended. Parking easy, and after eating, you can hop to one of the many cute cafes in 동편마을. Solid 4/5 for a casual Korean-Chinese fix.
