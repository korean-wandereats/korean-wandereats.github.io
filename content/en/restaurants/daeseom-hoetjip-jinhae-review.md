📍 **진해, Korea** · 💰 고급모듬회 특대 150,000원, 하모회 50,000원 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 예약

---

> *"Jinhae's raw fish spot that delivers the sea on a plate."*

Drove all the way to Jinhae for 대섬횟집 and it didn't disappoint. We ordered the 고급모듬회 특대 (150,000 won) and 하모회 (50,000 won) – enough for 5 people with all the seafood sides. The fish was so fresh it practically melted on the tongue. The 하모회 (conger eel) was silky and clean-tasting, perfect for summer. The view of the harbor from our window seat made everything taste better. Interior is spotless and quiet – great for bringing guests. Next time, I'm trying the 장어.

Reserve a window table – it's a must. The seafood platter that comes with the raw fish is insane: shrimp, abalone, sea squirt, you name it. For the price, you get quality and quantity. Not a 'hidden gem' – just a solid, honest place.
