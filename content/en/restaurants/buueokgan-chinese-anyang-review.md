---

> *"Solid Chinese-Korean that hits the spot without the guilt."*

Hit up 부엌간 차이니즈 in 동편마을 for a quick lunch. The 우삼겹소고기 짬뽕 surprised me—thick, meaty broth that actually had depth, not just salt. 수란 오겹 간짜장 was a touch sweet but clean, with real pork belly chunks. 흑초 등심 탕수육 (mini) was bigger than expected, and the black vinegar sauce balanced perfectly with tender pork. 어향군만두 was just okay, typical pan-fried dumplings.

Place is small, only a few tables, so weekday reservations help. After eating, walk to one of the many cafes in the village. Easy parking, chill vibes. Good for a weekend outing when you want Chinese without the greasy aftertaste.
