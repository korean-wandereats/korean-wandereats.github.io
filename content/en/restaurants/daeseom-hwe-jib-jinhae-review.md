📍 **진해, Korea** · 💰 고급모듬회 특대 150,000원, 하모회 50,000원 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 예약

---

> *"The ocean gave us its best, and we ate it all."*

Drove down to Jinhae for 대섬횟집 and it was worth every kilometer. We ordered the 고급모듬회 특대 (150,000 won) and 하모회 (50,000 won) – enough for five people to feast like royalty. The hwe was so fresh it barely needed ssamjang. Just a dab of washi and soy, and the fish melted on the tongue. The texture? Firm yet buttery. The 하모회 was a summer special – clean, light, with that subtle ocean sweetness.

The view is the real star. Window seats overlook the Jinhae bay – calm, blue, and distracting. Restaurant itself is spotless, quiet, and feels more like a private dining room. Reservation is a must, and request a window table. Next time I'm trying the jangeo (eel).
