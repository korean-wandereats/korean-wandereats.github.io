> *"Proper Korean-Chinese that hits the spot without the fuss."*

Went to Buheokgan Chinese in Anyang's Dongpyeon Village for a quick lunch. Ordered the soft egg pork belly jjajangmyeon, beef jjamppong, mini black vinegar tangsuyuk, and free shrimp dumplings from a review event. The jjajangmyeon was a bit sweet but clean, with generous pork belly chunks. The beef jjamppong surprised me—thick, meaty broth, not the usual watery stuff. Tangsuyuk had well-balanced black vinegar sauce and tender loin. Dumplings were just okay, standard fried ones.

The place is small, maybe 10 tables, so reserve on weekends. Parking was easy, and after eating, you can hop to one of the many cute cafes in the village. Solid 4/5 for a relaxed Korean-Chinese fix.
