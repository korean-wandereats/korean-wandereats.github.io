> *"Tender octopus that melts like butter, paired with a hip vibe and crisp white wine."*

Finally hit Ironside Fish & Oyster in Little Italy. The octopus a la plancha? Damn, that was soft — almost like Korean jangeo gui but with a perfect smoky char. Chopped tuna toast was fresh and crunchy, no fishy aftertaste. 

Pricey at $25 but the vibe and service make up for it. Reservation only, so plan ahead. White wine is a must. Solid 4/5 from this Korean traveler.
