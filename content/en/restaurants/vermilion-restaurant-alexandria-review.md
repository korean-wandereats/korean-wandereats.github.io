---

> *"Classic setting, creative food—Vermilion delivers polish without pretension."*

Walked into Vermilion and felt the Old Town charm—classy but not stiff, with a casual edge. Waiters were super polite, made me feel like a VIP. Ordered Bertties’s Buns (soft, pillowy), Slow Cooked Beef Short Rib (fall-apart tender, deep umami), and Grilled Cheshire Pork Porterhouse (smoky, juicy). For $50, the portions are fair—not a steal, but not a ripoff either.

Food was clean and solid, but not mind-blowing. The creativity on the menu is cool—twists on classics without going overboard. Next time I’d try the Farm Table tasting menu. Dress code leans smart casual; worth it for a date night or when you want to impress. Ambiance carries the experience.
