> *"A small, chef-run Chinese spot in Dongpyeon Village that nails clean flavors and generous portions."*

Went to Buheokgan Chinese in Anyang's Dongpyeon Village on a whim. Ordered the fried sauce noodles with pork belly and poached egg – slightly sweet but clean, with thick cuts of pork belly. The real surprise was the beef jjamppong: rich, thick broth with deep beef flavor, not your typical seafood-based version. The mini sweet and sour pork in black vinegar was perfectly balanced – tender meat, tangy sauce that wasn't cloying. The complimentary dumplings from a Naver review? Meh, standard pan-fried fare.

The place is small – only a few tables – so book ahead on weekdays. Parking was easy, and after eating, you can wander to one of the many cafes in the village. It's the kind of spot you'd bring a date or family for a relaxed weekend meal. Not groundbreaking, but solid and satisfying.
