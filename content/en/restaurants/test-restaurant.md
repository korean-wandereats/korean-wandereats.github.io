## First Impressions

Walked past three times before finally giving in to the line. Classic New York chaos - no seats, just a counter and a queue that moves faster than you'd expect.

## The Food

Ordered a plain cheese slice ($4). Coming from Korea where pizza is usually thick, sweet, and loaded with toppings, this was a shock - in a good way.

The crust has that thin, slightly charred bite that reminds me of Korean street food - simple but precise. The cheese ratio is perfect. Not greasy, not dry.

**Korean Palate Score:**
- Texture: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- Umami depth: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- Value: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- Solo traveler friendly: ✅

## The Honest Part

It\'s a slice of pizza. Not life-changing. But it\'s exactly what it promises to be - the best cheap slice in Manhattan. For $4, nothing comes close.

## Verdict

**Go.** Don\'t overthink it. Eat standing up. Leave.

**Price:** ~$4-8 per slice  
**Wait:** 5-15 mins  
**Location:** Greenwich Village, NYC
