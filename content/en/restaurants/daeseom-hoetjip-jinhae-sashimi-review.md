📍 **진해, Korea** · 💰 고급모듬회 특대 150,000원, 하모회 50,000원 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 예약

---

> *"The sea view is a bonus; the real star is the ridiculously fresh sashimi that comes with a mountain of seafood."*

Drove down to Jinhae for 대섬횟집 and honestly, the view alone is worth the trip. But the fish? Even better. We ordered the 고급모듬회 특대 (150,000원) and 하모회 (50,000원) – enough for 5 people to feast. The 모듬회 platter was insane: every slice had that perfect bounce, clean taste, no fishy smell. And they don't skimp on the banchan – whole abalone, sea squirt, raw shrimp… basically the ocean on a plate.

The 하모회 (conger eel) was the summer special and it did not disappoint. Silky, slightly chewy, with a dab of washi and soy – pure umami. Place is spotless, quiet, and the window seats are a must. Next time I'm trying the 장어 (grilled eel). Just book ahead and ask for a window table.
