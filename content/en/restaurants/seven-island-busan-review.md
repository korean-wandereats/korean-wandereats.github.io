📍 **Busan, Korea** · 💰 ₩8500 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ No wait

---

> *"Busan's most beautiful bakery with a view that hits harder than the ₩8,500 price tag."*

SEVEN ISLAND isn't just another ocean-view cafe—it's a 2025 Prix Versailles winner for a reason. The space is massive, airy, and every seat frames the sea. I came for the hype, stayed for the grapefruit millefeuille. That thing is a flaky, citrusy bomb—perfect balance of tart and sweet. The 크림 107겹 타워 (cream 107-layer tower) was a texture dream: custard and cream cheese stacked into buttery layers that shatter with each bite. Pepperoni pastry? Solid, but the potato bacon one stole my heart—soft, savory, satisfying. Brie sandwich was surprisingly good too, creamy and rich. 

Prices start at ₩8,500, which stings a bit, but portions are generous. No wait when I visited, but the place was packed—still plenty of seats thanks to the huge layout. Perfect for a date or family outing. If you're in Gadeokdo, make the trip. Just don't expect your wallet to thank you.
