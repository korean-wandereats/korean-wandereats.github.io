📍 **진해, Korea** · 💰 고급모듬회 특대 150,000원, 하모회 50,000원 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 예약

---

> *"You pay for the view, but the fish earns its keep."*

Rolled up to 대섬횟집 in Jinhae without a reservation—big mistake. Got lucky with a window seat overlooking the bay. Ordered the 고급모듬회 특대 (150,000 won) and 하모회 (50,000 won). The platter came loaded: slices of sea bream, flounder, and a whole army of shellfish. Texture was spot-on—firm, clean cuts that bounced back. The conger eel sashimi? Silky, mild, perfect for summer. Even the banchan felt premium: fresh seaweed, tangy pickles, and a smoky grilled fish on the side. Five of us ate like kings and still had leftovers. Only gripe: pricey for a casual meal. But for the quality and view? Fair trade. Next time, I'm trying the grilled eel. Book a window table—it's non-negotiable.
