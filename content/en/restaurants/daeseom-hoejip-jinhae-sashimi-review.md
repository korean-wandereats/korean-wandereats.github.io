📍 **진해, Korea** · 💰 고급모듬회 특대 150,000원, 하모회 50,000원 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ · ⏱️ 예약

---

> *"Sashimi so fresh it practically swims onto your plate."*

Rolled up to 대섬횟집 in Jinhae for the 고급모듬회 (150,000원) and 하모회 (50,000원). First bite of the 모듬회? That snap. Each slice firm, clean, with that ocean sweetness. 하모회 was silky, almost jelly-like—perfect for summer. The banchan spread was insane: raw shrimp, sea squirt, crab—all included. Five of us left stuffed.

Reserve a window table. That view of Jinhae Bay makes every bite better. Interior is spotless, vibe is calm. Next time I'm trying the 장어. No hype, just solid, fresh sashimi at a fair price.
