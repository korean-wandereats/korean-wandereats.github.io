---

> *"Excelsa coffee tastes more like tea, but I couldn't stop sipping."*

Sunday morning in DC, I waited 15 minutes for a seat at DUA DC Coffee. Hip interior, long benches, and a line out the door. I ordered their Excelsa pour-over from Indonesia—rare coffee that smells and tastes more like tea. Light, acidic, addictive. The avocado toast? Creamy but boring. Skip it.

If you want something different from your usual coffee, this is it. Not life-changing, but a fun detour. $10 for coffee and toast is fair for DC.
